Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Numyn (“Numyn”, “we”, “us”, or “our”) collects, uses, and protects your personal information in connection with:

  • The Numyn marketing website (including numyn.ca and any subdomains).
  • Our waitlist and early-access sign-up forms.
  • Emails and support requests you send to us.
  • Licensing, purchase, and account-related information.

Numyn is developed and operated by a sole proprietor based in Ontario, Canada. We aim to handle your personal information in line with applicable Canadian privacy requirements, including the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and any other provincial laws that may apply.

1. Scope and key concepts

This policy applies to personal information that we collect and control, such as information you provide through our website, waitlist forms, licensing system, or support channels. “Personal information” means information about an identifiable individual, such as a name, email address, or contact details.

Local bookkeeping data inside the Numyn desktop app is treated differently:

  • Numyn is designed as a primarily local desktop application. Your bookkeeping data (e.g., income, expenses, clients, suppliers) is stored on your own device by default.
  • We do not automatically receive or host your bookkeeping data on our own servers.
  • If cloud backup or sync features are introduced in the future, they will be clearly described, be optional, and this policy will be updated to explain how any additional data is handled.

2. What information we collect

2.1 Optional website analytics and technical information

On numyn.ca and app.numyn.ca, optional analytics starts only after you agree to the current analytics notice. Earlier approval for page counts does not authorise these expanded metrics: we ask again. Earlier declines remain respected until they expire or you change your choice. Declining does not affect website access, forms or services.

We send our own API the approved public page path, broad device category (mobile/tablet/desktop), browser and operating-system families without versions, a recognised referral source category, and a coarse page-load time range. Referral categories include Facebook, Instagram, Google, Bing, DuckDuckGo, LinkedIn, Reddit, YouTube and t.co. Other referring domains are grouped as “other”; absent referrals are “direct/unknown”; navigation within Numyn is “internal”. Full referring URLs, subdomains and query strings are not sent in analytics.

We count selected navigation actions to features, pricing, waitlist, contact, services and bookkeeping pages. When a consenting visitor successfully creates a new waitlist signup, the browser sends a separate signup-success count without the email, name, signup ID or form contents. Duplicate signups do not trigger this count. Analytics consent is separate from waitlist email consent.

We use page and action counts to improve content and navigation, referral totals to understand which channels bring interest, device/browser/OS categories to prioritise compatibility work, and load-time ranges to identify slow pages. We store separate daily totals by website and metric, and page-view totals by approved page, for a rolling 90 days in the active database. We do not store individual analytics events, visitor or session identifiers, raw user-agent strings, IP addresses, country or other location information, full URLs, form contents or browsing histories linked to subscriber records. We do not fingerprint visitors, sell analytics or use it for advertising.

Separate from optional analytics, our API server keeps operational access and error logs. These can contain IP addresses, request URLs (including query strings), timestamps, response details, referring URLs and browser user-agent information. These logs support troubleshooting and security; declining analytics does not prevent logs generated by ordinary requests to our services. System services may also write diagnostic records to system logs. The absence of IP addresses from the analytics database does not mean that our services never store IP addresses.

GitHub Pages hosts our static sites. Our API instance is hosted by Amazon Web Services in the Canada (Central) region in Montreal. This describes the instance location, not a guarantee that every provider processes all information exclusively in Canada. Providers may process information outside Canada, where it can be subject to local laws and lawful access by authorities.

Only authenticated administrators can view analytics reports. Broad categories and separate totals reduce identification risks, but small counts may still permit inferences; we restrict access and retention accordingly. Withdraw consent through Analytics preferences in the footer at any time to stop future collection. Existing aggregate counts cannot be linked back to you for individual removal. Global Privacy Control disables optional analytics. Do Not Track disables it by default; you may explicitly choose “Allow analytics despite Do Not Track” for this website. Choices are separate on each site.

2.2 Waitlist and early-access sign-ups

Website submissions also store the submission time, IP address information and browser user-agent supplied with the request. These records are separate from optional analytics and are not used to link analytics browsing activity to your email address.

If you join the Numyn waitlist or request early access, we may collect:

  • Your email address (required).
  • Your name and/or business name (optional).
  • Information about your business type or location, if you choose to share it (optional).

We use this information to manage the waitlist, contact you about early access opportunities, invite you to beta programs, and send updates about Numyn’s launch and related features.

If you no longer wish to receive waitlist or launch updates, you can unsubscribe at any time by using the unsubscribe form on our waitlist page or by contacting us at support@numyn.ca. If you unsubscribe, we will remove your email from the waitlist records, subject to any minimal retention that may be required for legal or technical reasons (for example, to honour your opt-out).

2.3 Purchases, licensing, and billing

When Numyn licenses are available for purchase, we plan to use a third-party payment and licensing provider (for example, Lemon Squeezy or a similar service). During checkout, that provider may collect information such as:

  • Name and contact information.
  • Billing address and country.
  • Payment method details (e.g., card information).

We do not receive or store your full payment card number on our own systems. We may receive limited purchase details from the provider—such as your name, email address, country, and license key— to create and manage your license, send receipts, and provide support.

2.4 Support and direct communications

Website submissions also store the submission time, IP address information and browser user-agent supplied with the request. These records are separate from optional analytics and are not used to link analytics browsing activity to your email address.

If you email us (for example, at support@numyn.ca) or contact us through a form, we may collect:

  • Your name and email address.
  • The content of your message and any attachments you choose to send.
  • Technical details you provide to help troubleshoot an issue (e.g., operating system, Numyn version).

3. How we use your information

We use personal information for purposes such as:

  • Providing access to Numyn and managing licensing and activation.
  • Sending waitlist updates, launch announcements, and product information (with your consent).
  • Responding to support requests, bug reports, and feature feedback.
  • Sending important notices about security, compatibility, or critical product changes.
  • Operating, maintaining, and improving our website and services.
  • Keeping business and accounting records as required by law.

For marketing-type emails (like waitlist updates and launch news), we rely on your express consent. You can withdraw that consent at any time by using an unsubscribe option provided in the email (where available) or through the unsubscribe form on our waitlist page, or by contacting us directly.

4. Consent

We seek meaningful consent for the collection, use and disclosure of personal information unless applicable law permits or requires otherwise. Optional website analytics requires an affirmative choice. You may withdraw consent using Analytics preferences. Consent to analytics is separate from consent to waitlist or promotional emails.

5. Cookies and similar technologies

Optional analytics uses no tracking cookies or persistent visitor IDs. We save only your analytics choice, whether you explicitly overrode Do Not Track, and the choice date in local storage on your device for up to 180 days before asking again. This preference is not sent to us as an identifier. Theme preferences may also be saved locally. Clearing browser storage resets these choices.

6. How we share information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited personal information with third parties in these situations:

  • Service providers – such as payment processors, email service providers, website hosting, and error-reporting or analytics tools used to operate Numyn and the website.
  • Business or legal obligations – if required by law, regulation, court order, or in response to lawful requests by public authorities.
  • Business changes – in the event of a reorganization, sale, or other business transaction involving Numyn, where personal information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to applicable laws.

Some of these service providers may be located outside of Canada (for example, in the United States or the European Union), which means your information may be subject to the laws of those jurisdictions.

7. Data retention

The 90-day analytics period applies to active analytics totals, not all operational records. Web-server logs are configured to rotate daily and retain up to 14 rotated files; this is not an exact 14-day deletion deadline. Other application and system logs do not currently have a verified fixed time-based deletion schedule and may remain longer. We are reviewing these settings to establish appropriate retention limits.

Automatic snapshots of the API instance are currently disabled. This does not establish that no manual snapshots or other backups exist. Any backup copies require separate retention and access controls; we do not represent the active analytics deletion period as a verified deletion deadline for every backup.

We keep your personal information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes set out in this policy, including:

  • Maintaining your license and purchase history.
  • Responding to support requests and tracking ongoing issues.
  • Complying with tax, accounting, and other legal requirements.

If you ask us to delete certain information, we will do so where possible, subject to any legal obligations to retain records for a longer period (for example, for tax or audit purposes).

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the personal information we control from unauthorized access, use, or disclosure. However, no online or email transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of information transmitted over the Internet.

You are responsible for safeguarding your own devices and any local Numyn data stored on them, including using appropriate passwords, encryption, and backups where applicable.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights such as:

  • The right to request access to the personal information we hold about you.
  • The right to request correction or updates to inaccurate or incomplete information.
  • The right to withdraw consent to marketing communications at any time.
  • In some cases, the right to request deletion of personal information, subject to legal or contractual restrictions.

For waitlist and launch-update emails, you can withdraw your consent at any time by unsubscribing using the form on our waitlist page or by contacting us at support@numyn.ca. We will respect your choice and stop sending those communications, subject to any necessary confirmation emails or records of your opt-out.

To make a request, contact us at support@numyn.ca. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling certain requests.

10. Children’s privacy

Numyn is intended for adult users who operate or manage a business. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, please contact us so we can take appropriate steps to delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, services, or legal obligations. When we do, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. In cases of material changes, we may provide additional notice (for example, via the website or by email, where appropriate).
